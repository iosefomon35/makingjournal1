## Final Project Summary

My project is a digital mobile application designed to help address loneliness and improve user wellbeing through connection and supportive interaction. The target users are individuals who may feel socially isolated or disconnected and are looking for simple, accessible ways to engage with others or feel supported.

The main purpose of the app is to provide a safe and easy-to-use platform that encourages positive connection and reduces feelings of loneliness. The design focuses on simplicity, clarity, and emotional accessibility to ensure users can navigate the app without confusion or barriers.

I developed this project using Figma, iterating through multiple design stages based on peer feedback and self-evaluation. Early feedback highlighted issues with navigation and onboarding, which led me to simplify the user flow and improve clarity for first-time users. I also refined the visual design by adjusting typography, spacing, and layout hierarchy to improve readability and user experience.

The final outcome is a more refined and user-friendly interface that clearly communicates its purpose. The project demonstrates my ability to respond to feedback, improve usability, and develop a concept into a functional digital design solution focused on wellbeing.
